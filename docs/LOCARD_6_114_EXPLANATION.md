## Scope and authority

`forensic_assistant/investigation_ai/explanation.py` adds a read-only backend and
bounded presentation pages. It performs no model request, forensic database lookup,
authorization change, transcript append, sidecar write or report import. It is not
D1b live persistence, D2 model reasoning or D3 hierarchical synthesis.

`open_history(root, identity)` validates the transcript through existing C1/B2 and
S1 reconstruction services, checks manifest stability before/after, and creates a
private validated session. Arbitrary serialized findings are not a public input.
The service reuses accepted original support rather than reading raw case records.
`page(session, cursor)` assembles one bounded structured page; `render(page)` is
presentation only. Rendering JSON supplied independently is not evidence validation.

The session retains references to existing bounded S1/C1 results, not another copy
of the evidence store or entire graph per page. Unit-level seals detect changed
source objects. Original support and read-view bindings are checked again at the
adapter boundary. The backend is independent of the CLI and usable by another local
presentation surface without parsing human output.

## Contracts and templates

| Contract | Role |
|---|---|
| `investigative-statement-v1` | Application identity, run/view, classification, exact source units/dependencies, origin/template, qualifications, objective associations and inclusion status |
| `investigative-section-v1` | Registered display section with bounded statement references |
| `integrated-explanation-v1` | Original question, original assessment status/digest, bound source revision, summary references, sections, coverage, omissions and continuation cursor |
| `deterministic-explanation-v1` | Versioned assembly/wording/order rule |

Evidence-backed statements are only `recorded_observation`,
`deterministic_interpretation` or `validated_relationship`. Other classes are
`unverified_hypothesis`, `unverified_explanation`, `unresolved_question`,
`coverage_limitation` and `recommended_action`.

`_fact_text` uses S1's registered predicate subjects and exact JSON-quoted label/value
wording, with artifact-specific browser/Registry subjects for the generic predicate.
The ASR subject comes from the existing `defender-asr-block-v1` rule. It is never
generated by a model. Required S1 qualifications are carried with every statement.
Original timestamp metadata, source context, truncation, provenance and delta lineage
remain in dependency diagnostics. A premise digest is a reference to validated
support, not a substitute for original evidence in any future model request.

Relationship wording is limited to the four existing S2 predicates. The service
recomputes the registered comparison using accepted endpoint clauses and verifies
the relationship ID and qualifications. It preserves recorded-coordinate semantics,
unknown clock synchronization, source uncertainty and current/historical frontier
metadata. Equal paths do not establish object identity. Historical derivations have
their own section and the sparse-membership caveat.

No compound causal sentence is created by combining unrelated observations. Values
that themselves contain assertions remain quoted data. No model-authored connective
enters factual wording. All untrusted strings are escaped for terminal output.

## Question, summary and proposals

The original question is retained verbatim in every JSON page. The original S3
status and assessment digest appear near the beginning; the complete unchanged
assessment is a separately paginated body item. Provisional S4-A objectives retain
their source spans, polarity, technical bindings, unresolved roles and assessment
objects. Only established registered direct-support associations attach factual
statements to objectives; no lexical guess repairs an unresolved objective.

The first page's executive summary references up to four already included factual
body atoms with all their qualifications, under a 2-KiB text/qualification selection
budget. It does not paraphrase them. All other factual body items are counted as
not selected for that summary; omission from the summary does not imply irrelevance.
Ordering is deterministic: recognized interpretations, observations, current and
historical relationships, then unverified/objective/coverage sections, with stable
source-ID tie breaks. This is a presentation order, not a relevance finding.

Analysis hypotheses keep record-only support where that is all their source provides.
B2 proposals retain exact request/packet origin, payload, alternatives, uncertainty,
binding and request-local alias legends. Their citations confer no factual authority.
Model questions are grouped for display but not semantically merged. Exact duplicate
accounting is inherited from C1; original entries remain accessible and repetition
does not increase confidence. Recommended actions remain unverified suggestions,
not executed tools or permission to retrieve anything.

## Bounded pages and cursors

- Full serialized JSON page: at most **16,384 UTF-8 bytes**. Human-rendered page is
  independently checked against the same byte ceiling.
- At most **64 body statement references per page**; summary references point to
  those same atoms rather than additional statements.
- At most **192 candidate admissions** per assembly traversal. Larger inventories
  expose the unprocessed tail as an assembly-limit omission; no hidden restart.
- Whole atoms that cannot fit their support/qualification envelope are omitted with
  a source ID and reason, never truncated into misleading facts.

Page counts distinguish included/omitted items on that page, pending candidates,
previously traversed candidates and the global admission-limit tail. The final page
marks `assembly_exhausted`, not forensic completeness. Consumers summing page results
must not count the global admission-limit tail more than once. Original coverage
and source organization stop reasons remain visible.

Cursors encode an offset and a binding digest covering run, read view, transcript
chain, organization revision/digest, explanation rules and canonical candidate
ordering. Invalid or stale bindings fail closed. A cursor is navigation metadata,
not a security credential or authorization. New source state requires reconstruction
and a new cursor. Unknown C1 v1 profiles retain the existing original-reader boundary.

No C1 storage/resource ceiling was enlarged. Exact provenance consumes substantial
page space. This implementation favors complete per-page support over compact JSON;
it does not intern a new shared evidence dictionary or change source contracts.

The human command streams all bounded pages sequentially. It does not build one

For the next JSON page, append `--cursor "<next_cursor from the preceding page>"`
to the same command. `--cursor` requires `--explanation`. Do not substitute another
run, source revision or reader profile. Human output can also start at that cursor.

explanation was not persisted during the historical investigation. Neither recorded
read-view integrity nor transcript hashes establish current-case equality or parent
process cleanup. Existing legacy report-import restrictions are untouched.

## C1 prospective checkpoint gate

Added `test_c1_prospective_failure_gate` scenarios for failure after a committed
checkpoint, failure after a durable B2 decision, final-anchor/checkpoint failure,
and one interrupt during a later pending update. Tests check prior digest retention,
exact replay, primary call/lookup ceilings, closure and ten-step ceiling. A separate
test directly excludes rejected analysis from C1 factual lineage. Existing C1 tests
continue to cover primary journal reservations and final operation reservations.

**Gate remains open:** when the final `organization_final` anchor append fails,
the prior organization digest is preserved and a processing-failure stop is recorded,
but existing C1 reconstruction can still report `incomplete=false`. The new regression
is a strict expected failure. D1a carries source processing stops into its preview;
it does not repair or reinterpret C1 checkpoints. Future D1b must not be declared
ready until that reporting defect is corrected and its strict test passes.

Cancellation injection is one interrupt, not repeated interrupts during finalization.
It allows bounded finalization of accepted state; a completed local organization of
that partial accepted state is distinct from completed evidence examination. Tests
do not claim power-loss/fsync atomicity or parent cleanup beyond recorded observations.

## Limitations and acceptance

This is a factual explanation spine, not new forensic reasoning. Summary selection
is deliberately conservative, exact questions are not semantically merged, and
complete provenance produces 24 small JSON pages for the six-record historical run.
The human command streams them without manual cursor copying. Future presentation
compression must preserve exact support and remains separate work.

Manual review should verify readable Browser/MFT/Prefetch/Defender statements, the
ASR blocking qualification, separate current/historical chronology, original question
status and uncertainty. The preview must not assert that a ZIP caused later activity
or that the referenced PowerShell script successfully executed. It must not imply
that B2 synthesis packets represented all examined records.

No D1b persistence, D2 provider proposals, additional authorization, live evidence
access, forensic report import, restart continuation or new inference rules are
implemented. No commit or push was made.
